# Safety and scams — Cameroon rentals (RAG knowledge)

Use this content to warn users and answer “is this safe?” style questions. Tone: practical, non-alarmist.

## Red flags before paying

- Pressure to pay **large cash deposits** before you have seen the property in person or via a trusted video walkthrough.
- Listings with **prices far below** similar homes in the same area with no clear reason.
- Requests to send money to **personal mobile money** without a written agreement or platform record.
- **No verifiable landlord** profile or refusal to communicate through RoomBay messaging.

## Safer practices

- Prefer **verified** landlords and listings when available.
- **Visit in daylight** with a friend when possible; confirm the address matches the listing.
- Keep **proof of payment** (screenshots, receipts) aligned with your lease reference.
- Use **in-app messaging** for the main conversation trail; avoid switching to opaque channels too early.

## If something feels wrong

- Stop the transaction, **report the listing or user** through RoomBay, and keep screenshots of conversations and payment attempts.
- Local police or consumer protection channels apply offline; RoomBay can moderate accounts and listings on-platform.
