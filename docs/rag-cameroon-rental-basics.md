# Cameroon rental context (RAG knowledge)

Short facts the assistant can cite. Currency: **FCFA (XAF)**. Major cities include **Douala**, **Yaoundé**, **Bafoussam**, **Buea**, **Limbe**.

## Typical rental types

- **Chambre / studio**: single room or small studio; often most affordable for students and young workers.
- **Appartement**: multi-room apartment in a building or compound.
- **Maison**: standalone house; may include yard; varies widely by neighbourhood.

## What tenants usually clarify before visiting

- Monthly rent, **deposit (caution)**, and **agency fees** if any.
- Whether **utilities** (water, electricity, generator schedule) are included or metered separately.
- **Minimum lease duration** and notice period.
- **House rules**: guests, quiet hours, shared kitchen/bathroom in coliving setups.

## Location and commute

- Ask about proximity to work/university, **motor taxi** or bus access, and security of the street at night.
- Neighbourhoods differ in price and noise; “near the main road” can mean more traffic and dust.

## Documentation norms (informal market)

- Many landlords use a simple **lease (bail)** or written agreement; always request a copy and receipts for deposits and rent paid.
- ID copies may be requested; share only what you are comfortable with and prefer verified landlords on RoomBay when possible.
