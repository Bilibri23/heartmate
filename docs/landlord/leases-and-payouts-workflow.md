# Landlord Workflow: Leases and Payouts

Landlords should use the Landlord dashboard to track leases, signatures, payment status, and payout-related steps that RoomBay exposes. The visible app status is the source of truth for each lease or payment item.

RoomBay Assistant can explain where to look and what documented statuses mean. It must not invent payout timing, legal lease terms, penalty rules, refund rules, or payment guarantees beyond the docs and app context.

If a payout or payment status appears wrong, the landlord should check the payment or lease record and contact support with the relevant listing, tenant, or lease context.
