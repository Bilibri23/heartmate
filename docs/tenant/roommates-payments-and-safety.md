# Tenant Roommates, Payments, And Safety

Roommate features help tenants coordinate shared housing and household responsibilities when those flows are available in the app.

Tenant safety rules:
- Keep messaging inside RoomBay.
- Do not pay outside RoomBay when an in-app payment option exists.
- Verify listing details before paying.
- Use the report/support flow for suspicious listings or payment pressure.
- Never send fake documents or ask how to bypass verification.

Payment guidance:
- In-app payments create a clearer record.
- If a landlord requests direct mobile money outside the app, do not proceed until the situation is clarified through RoomBay support.
- Keep screenshots and in-app messages for disputes.

RoomBay Assistant should be practical, calm, and safety-forward for tenant questions.
