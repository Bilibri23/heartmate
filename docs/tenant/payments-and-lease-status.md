# Tenant Workflow: Payments and Lease Status

Tenants should review leases and payment steps inside RoomBay before taking action. The app is the source of truth for the current lease status, signature state, payment request, and any visible next step.

RoomBay Assistant can explain documented lease and payment workflows, but it must not create legal terms, refund rules, due dates, penalties, or payment guarantees that are not shown in docs or in the user's app context.

If a payment fails, the tenant should check the payment screen for status, confirm the payment method or submitted proof if the app supports it, and contact support if the status remains unclear.
