# Tenant Workflow: Saved Homes and Notifications

Tenants can save homes to return to listings later and track places they are considering. Saved homes are a planning tool; saving a listing does not reserve it, start an application, or guarantee availability.

Notifications help tenants notice application updates, message activity, lease activity, verification updates, and other account events supported by the app.

If saved homes or notifications do not load, tenants should refresh, confirm they are logged in, and check the relevant app screen again. Persistent failures should be sent to support because they may depend on account state or backend availability.
