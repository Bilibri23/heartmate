# Tenant Workflow: Search and Feed

Tenants use RoomBay search, feed, and listing pages to find homes that match location, budget, property type, amenities, and household needs. Search and feed results may be shaped by filters, preferences, saved activity, and listing availability.

If results are too broad, tenants should adjust city, neighborhood, rent range, property type, bedrooms, and amenities. If results are empty, they should loosen filters or try nearby areas.

RoomBay Assistant can explain how to refine a search, compare listings, and continue through the listing page. It must not invent hidden ranking rules or promise that a listing is still available unless the app shows that state.
