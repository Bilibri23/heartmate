# ✅ Verification Page - Role-Specific Content FIXED!

## 🎯 What Was Wrong

The "What Happens Next?" section was showing student-specific text for both roles:
- ❌ Landlords saw: "verify your student ID"
- ❌ Landlords saw: "Start applying to listings and messaging landlords!"

## ✅ What's Fixed

Now the page is **completely role-specific** from top to bottom!

---

## 📊 Student vs Landlord Verification

### **STUDENT VERIFICATION:**

#### **Header:**
```
Student Verification
Verify your student status to access all features
```

#### **Why Verify:**
- ✅ Apply to listings and message landlords
- ✅ Get a verified badge on your profile
- ✅ Build trust in the community
- ✅ Priority in landlord responses

#### **Form Fields:**
1. University (autocomplete search)
2. Student ID Number
3. Student ID Photo (1 upload)

**Total: 3 fields**

#### **What Happens Next:**
1. **We Review Your Submission**
   - Our team will verify your **student ID** within 24-48 hours

2. **You Get Notified**
   - We'll send you an email when verification is complete

3. **Full Access Unlocked**
   - Start **applying to listings and messaging landlords!**

---

### **LANDLORD VERIFICATION:**

#### **Header:**
```
Landlord Verification
Verify your identity to post listings and build trust
```

#### **Why Verify:**
- ✅ Post listings and message students
- ✅ Get a verified badge on your profile
- ✅ Build trust in the community
- ✅ Higher visibility in search results

#### **Form Fields:**
1. ID Document Type (dropdown)
   - National ID Card (CNI)
   - Passport
   - Driver's License
   - Voter's Card
   - Residence Permit

2. ID Number
3. ID Front Photo
4. ID Back Photo (optional)
5. Selfie with ID
6. Address Line 1
7. Address Line 2 (optional)
8. City
9. Region

**Total: 9 fields (7 required)**

#### **What Happens Next:**
1. **We Review Your Submission**
   - Our team will verify your **identity documents** within 24-48 hours

2. **You Get Notified**
   - We'll send you an email when verification is complete

3. **Full Access Unlocked**
   - Start **posting listings and messaging students!**
